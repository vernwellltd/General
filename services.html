Most BI consultants are technical specialists who build what they're told. Defined project (build a dashboard, optimise a slow report, redesign a data structure) or retained monthly support. All work is fixed price once scoped.

Both. Vernwell works with Tableau, Power BI, and builds directly in SQL where a separate reporting layer isn't needed. The tool recommendation depends on what you already have and what the team can maintain — not a preference for one platform.
We already have a BI team — can Vernwell work alongside them?
Yes, this is common. Vernwell often works as a senior resource alongside an existing team — handling more complex or strategic work, providing a second opinion on architecture decisions, or picking up specific projects the internal team doesn't have capacity for.

Usually with a short conversation to understand the problem and whether there's a good fit. From there, most projects start with a short scoping phase — a few days to understand the data environment and define exactly what needs building, priced separately from the build itself.
02

AI Automation

Practical AI workflows that save real time in real processes — not proof-of-concepts that never reach production.

Make.com Zapier Relevance AI Voiceflow OpenAI API

What gets automated

  • Document intake and processing — extracting structured data from invoices, contracts and forms
  • Customer-facing communications — AI-assisted responses and lead qualification workflows
  • Internal knowledge bases and staff chatbots
  • Data pipeline automation — moving and transforming data between systems automatically
  • Reporting triggers and alerts — the right information surfaced at the right time

Who this is for

SMEs with growing operational complexity who don't have a dedicated technology team. Professional services businesses — estate agents, solicitors, recruitment firms, accountants — where significant time is spent on repetitive document handling. And businesses going through growth where manual processes that worked at 20 people are starting to break at 50 or 100.

Typical cost and timeline

A well-scoped single workflow — invoice processing automation or lead qualification bot — typically takes 2–4 weeks and costs in the range of £2,000–£6,000 depending on complexity. All work is fixed price once scoped.

We don't know where to start with AI — can Vernwell help with that?
Yes — a common starting point is a short process audit: identifying which processes consume the most manual time and which are technically viable to automate. This produces a prioritised shortlist so you know exactly where to start and what the realistic time savings look like, before committing to any build work.
Will we be able to maintain it ourselves after it's built?
Yes — that's part of the brief. Tools like Make.com and Zapier are designed for non-technical maintenance. Vernwell builds with handover in mind: documentation, a walkthrough session, and a short support period included in every project.
