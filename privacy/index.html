Privacy Policy

What information we collect

When you submit the contact form on this website, we collect the information you provide: your name, company name, email address, phone number, and the content of your message. We do not collect any information automatically beyond standard website analytics.

How we use your information

Information submitted via the contact form is used solely to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, except where required to operate the contact form itself (via Formspree) or where required by law.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Analytics data is aggregated and anonymised where possible. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using your browser settings or available opt-out extensions.

Data retention

Enquiry information is retained only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and maintain a record of business correspondence, in line with normal commercial practice.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To make a request, contact james.eyles@vernwell.co.uk.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to james.eyles@vernwell.co.uk.
